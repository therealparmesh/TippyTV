Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

TippyTV plays live TV from sources you configure. TippyTV has no central user account, advertising, or in-app analytics. This policy describes TippyTV. The app store and the services you choose operate under their own privacy policies.

Your TV stores your profile details, including server addresses, playlists and guide URLs, and the tokens or credentials needed to reconnect. TippyTV keeps these profiles and saved lineups encrypted on the TV. Settings and playlist favorites stay on the TV. A Jellyfin server stores Jellyfin favorites in your account. The programme guide stays in memory while the app runs.

TippyTV contacts the servers and files you choose to sign in, load channels and listings, and play video. These services receive the information needed to complete each request, which can include login credentials, account and device identifiers, and your network address. Use HTTPS addresses where your sources support them. Requests to an HTTP source can be read by others with access to that network connection.

Optional phone setup sends the form directly from your phone to the TV over HTTPS. To obtain a trusted certificate, Lancert publishes a DNS name that points to the private IP address of your TV, and Let’s Encrypt issues the certificate. The name and private IP are publicly resolvable, and the name may appear in public certificate logs. Your form entries go to the TV, and the certificate private key stays there. You can set up a source with the remote instead.

Remove a profile or sign out to clear its local profile and saved lineup. Removing a playlist or Xtream profile also removes its local favorites. Clear TippyTV’s app data or uninstall it to remove remaining local data, including phone setup certificates. Data held by a source or certificate service is handled by that service.

For privacy questions, contact the TippyTV maintainer at the TippyTV issue tracker. Please keep passwords, access tokens, and private playlist links out of public issues.
