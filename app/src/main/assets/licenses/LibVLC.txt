LibVLC is dynamically linked. TippyTV's source and Gradle build permit rebuilding
with a modified LibVLC AAR. The build recipe and HLS playlist-duration fix are:
https://github.com/therealparmesh/TippyTV/blob/main/tools/build-libvlc.sh
https://github.com/therealparmesh/TippyTV/blob/main/third_party/libvlc/hls-duration.patch
The modification supplies the target segment duration when updating a live HLS
playlist, allowing the library to account for skipped segments without an assertion.
Run tools/build-libvlc.sh to rebuild; the app links build/libvlc/libvlc-patched.aar.
