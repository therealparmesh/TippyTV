mpv / FFmpeg and their contributors

This application includes unmodified native libraries from mpv-android 2026-09-17.
The native build enables GPLv3 components. See GPL-3.0.txt and the corresponding
source repositories for component copyrights and additional license notices.
The CA bundle is distributed by the upstream mpv-android project.

Native mpv source:
https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/tree/0b7ed670f7c353dd3dd4f8ae0fc788a181a15aa6
Native FFmpeg source:
https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/094a2f8a2a5e7fa64736e067de224ce28fdf5979
Complete native dependency versions:
https://github.com/mpv-android/mpv-android/releases/tag/2026-09-17
Source download and build scripts for all bundled native dependencies:
https://github.com/mpv-android/mpv-android/tree/fdf74f6830c47dbaa8a22ac79726e8303f1db5af/buildscripts

TippyTV source, native bridge, dependency preparation and build instructions:
https://github.com/therealparmesh/TippyTV
https://github.com/therealparmesh/TippyTV/blob/main/third_party/mpv/README.md

The upstream Android app and its singleton JNI wrapper are not included.
The Android surface/JVM integration follows the upstream project's MIT-licensed
example; its notice is in mpv-android-MIT.txt.
